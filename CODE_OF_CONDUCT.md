# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what’s best for the project

Examples of behavior that isn’t okay:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Publishing others’ private info without permission

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainer via GitHub (open a private security advisory if it’s sensitive, otherwise an issue or discussion). Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block people who break this.
